Work // 2022 – present

Research at sea and in the forest

Twelve research roles across the Philippines, the UK and Europe — in marine and terrestrial ecosystems, with one question running through them: how do human activities affect wildlife, and what can be done about it locally?

Flagship project // 2026 – present

The Search for Luzon’s Lost Bird

My own project: the status and distribution of the Luzon Buttonquail (Turnix worcesteri) and Luzon Rail (Lewinia mirifica), two poorly documented, data-deficient Philippine endemics.

  • RolePrincipal Investigator & Project Lead
  • FundingOriental Bird Club Conservation Grant
  • PartnersKalahan Educational Foundation, NTFP-EP Asia, Municipality of Santa Fe

A — At sea

Cetaceans

Boat-based and land-based surveys, photo-identification, acoustic monitoring and stranding response, in the Philippines, Wales, Spain and Denmark.

  • 2024 – 2025

    Irrawaddy dolphins & offshore wind

    GEOSPHERE Technologies, Inc.
    Bicol, Philippines

    Marine Mammal Surveyor and Researcher. Monthly offshore and coastal surveys for the Philippines’ first offshore wind farm: dolphin habitat use, distribution and behaviour, fisher interviews, and mitigation recommendations.

  • 2024

    Humpback whale expeditions

    Balyena.org
    Camiguin Island, Babuyan Islands, Philippines

    Research intern. Surveys and photo-identification; digitised 20 years of cetacean sighting records; co-authored a poster for the 2024 Society for Marine Mammalogy Conference; assisted a live stranding response and a sperm whale necropsy.

  • 2023

    Porpoise bycatch & acoustic deterrents

    Deutsches Meeresmuseum & Thünen Institute
    Fyns Hoved, Denmark

    Research intern on the PAL-CE project. Land-based behavioural observations with theodolite tracking and drone footage, hydrophone recordings, and field data management.

  • 2023

    Cetacean research expeditions

    CIRCE — Conservation, Information and Study of Cetaceans
    El Pelayo, Spain

    Research intern. Survey expeditions, photographic data collection for photo-identification, and field equipment maintenance.

  • 2022

    Cetacean surveys, photo-ID & outreach

    Sea Watch Foundation
    New Quay, Wales

    Research intern, then Educational and Outreach Assistant. Boat-based and landwatch surveys, photo-identification and data management; ran the visitor centre and delivered marine conservation workshops.

B — In the forest

Birds & biodiversity

Ornithological fieldwork and biodiversity monitoring across Luzon, Mindoro, Biliran and Bicol — plus mammals, bats and herpetofauna along the way.

  • 2026 – present

    The Search for Luzon’s Lost Bird

    Principal Investigator & Project Lead
    Nueva Vizcaya, Philippines

    Transects, camera traps, passive acoustic monitoring, mist-netting and community interviews for Luzon Buttonquail and Luzon Rail. Presence of Luzon Rail documented.

  • 2026

    Thesis field surveys

    Polytechnic University of the Philippines
    General Nakar, Philippines

    Research Technician. Ornithological surveys and biodiversity assessments for undergraduate thesis research; helped standardise survey methods and guided fieldwork preparation.

  • 2026

    Arboreal mammals & disturbance

    Newcastle University
    Subic, Philippines

    Research Assistant. Postgraduate field research on arboreal mammal habitat use; translation and coordination with local guides; guidance on culturally appropriate, equitable research practice.

  • 2026

    Montane bird surveys & field guide

    Biodiversity Foundation of the Philippines, Inc.
    Biliran, Philippines

    Research Assistant – Ornithologist. Line transects, point counts and mist-netting in remote montane habitat; data and photographs contributed to a local flora and fauna field guide.

  • 2025

    Avian & bat sampling

    GRIDS, Inc.
    Mindoro, Philippines

    Junior Biodiversity Specialist (Avian). Twice-daily transect surveys, mist-netting of birds and bats, and small mammal and herpetofaunal trapping.

  • 2024 – 2025

    Migratory birds & offshore wind

    NIRAS
    Bicol, Philippines

    Researcher – Ornithology. Twice-monthly, 12-hour vantage point surveys over 12 months: raptors, seabirds, shorebirds and species of conservation concern around a proposed wind farm.
