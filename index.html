01 — About

From San Pablo City in the Philippines to the survey boats of Wales, Spain and Denmark — and back to Luzon’s forests. My work follows whales, dolphins and elusive birds, and the people who live alongside them.

I graduated in Ecology and Conservation from Lancaster University and I’m now pursuing an MSc in Marine Mammal Science at the University of St Andrews.

I’m most interested in practical, locally driven conservation: research that strengthens capacity in the Philippines and bridges science and communities.

03 — Research

Two threads, one question

How do human activities affect wildlife — and what can be done locally to protect it?

At sea

Cetaceans

Boat-based surveys, photo-identification, acoustics and stranding response — from Irrawaddy dolphins to humpback whales and harbour porpoises.

  • Irrawaddy dolphins & offshore wind Bicol // 2024–25
  • Humpback whale expeditions Camiguin // 2024
  • Porpoise bycatch & acoustics Denmark // 2023
Cetacean research

In the forest

Birds & biodiversity

Transects, point counts, vantage-point watches and mist-netting — documenting birds from migratory raptors to data-deficient endemics.

  • Luzon’s Lost Bird Project Luzon // 2026–
  • Montane bird surveys Biliran // 2026
  • Migratory birds & offshore wind Bicol // 2024–25
