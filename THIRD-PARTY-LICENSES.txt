Third-party material used on this site
=======================================

Photographs
-----------
All photographs are (c) Kenneth Manuel / Peliken.

Icons
-----
Line icons are from Lucide (https://lucide.dev), ISC licence; the licence text is in src/icons/LICENSE-lucide.txt.
The whale icon is from Lucide Lab (@lucide/lab), ISC licence.

Fonts (fonts/)
--------------
Fraunces, IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Sans, self-hosted, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

World map outlines
------------------
Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain, delivered as TopoJSON via the
world-atlas package v2.0.2 (https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas, ISC licence). The Journey and home-page maps are
drawn from these files; no tiles or external map services are used.

Background silhouettes
----------------------
Animal silhouettes come from PhyloPic (https://www.phylopic.org), public domain (PDM 1.0) or CC0 1.0, except the
Irrawaddy dolphin (Orcaella brevirostris) by Chris huh, CC BY-SA 3.0. Authors, where credited, include Margot Michaud,
Steven Traver, Ferran Sayol, Michael Day, Anthony Caravaggi, Andy Wilson, Matthew Speight, M Kolmann and
Mathieu Pelissie. Source records for every silhouette are listed in src/sil/_sources.txt. The branch and vine
drawings are original.

Textures
--------
Forest leaf-litter texture: Poly Haven 'forest_leaves_03' (CC0). Sea-foam texture: ambientCG 'Foam001' (CC0).

(https://xeno-canto.org) and Wikimedia Commons. Most Xeno-canto licences are non-commercial (CC BY-NC-SA / BY-NC-ND):
if the site is ever used commercially, replace them with the owner's own recordings.
Xeno-canto numbers: XC23085, XC442776, XC403744, XC812270, XC281900, XC542842, XC432231, XC477972, XC476785, XC308912,

XC791864, XC804507, XC346389, XC269959. The Xeno-canto page for each number gives the full credit.

Field recordings, credited under the player on each stop. Xeno-canto soundscapes XC563580, XC762388, XC313786,
